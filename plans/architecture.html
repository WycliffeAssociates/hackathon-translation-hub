  • No writes to the Forgejo volume or database. The mount is :ro,z.
  • No private repos. is_private = 0 is the rule.

  • No webhook, no inotify and no Forgejo HTTP API.
  • No full-text search over Bible text. No FTS5 and no tantivy.
  • No stored lint history. History for one repo can come later, on demand.
